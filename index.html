First 10 minutes with your pool

Six Play Boosters, a foil promo, and basic lands. Build at least 40 cards; these are strong starting numbers, then adjust to your pool.

01

Sort, then choose

Separate by color, multicolor, colorless, and lands. Identify your best threats and removal before chasing a theme. Usually play your two deepest colors.

02

Start with 17 lands

Try 23 spells and 17 lands. Prioritize early plays: Wizards' sample curve has 7–8 two-drops and 5–6 three-drops. Splash a third color only with real fixing and a worthwhile payoff.

03

Keep the sideboard close

All opened cards outside your deck are sideboard options. After game one, swap in extra removal, artifact answers, or a faster plan for the matchup.

Ten plans, one pool

Signpost commons and uncommons highlighted by Wizards, plus support to watch for. Treat them as clues about your opened pool, not a reason to force a color pair.

WHITE / BLUE · WU

Fatehold · surveil aggro

Signposts: Fatehold Chronologist, Prudent Fateseer. Look for: Semester Foreseer, Desperate Futurescribe; scry and surveil to turn on payoffs.

BLUE / BLACK · UB

Theorix · graveyard math

Signposts: Paradox Shaper, Theorix Metamage. Look for: cheap spells, self-mill, Void Extrapolator; seven cards in your graveyard matters.

BLACK / RED · BR

Stingerquill · direct damage

Signposts: Stingerquill Voxmancer, Whiplash Wordsmith. Look for: Essence Burn, Stingerquill Charm, Grim Repriser; noncombat damage drives the deck.

RED / GREEN · RG

Konstrari · Heartwood ramp

Signposts: Konstrari Improviser, Woodwork Prodigy. Look for: Heartwood makers and large finishers such as Archive Arbiter.

GREEN / WHITE · GW

Vigorbloom · life and counters

Signposts: Emergency Phytomedic, Vigorbloom Vanguard. Look for: repeatable life gain, Graft Surgeon, Bloombrute, +1/+1 counter payoffs.

WHITE / BLACK · WB

Liliana · attrition

Signposts: Edgar, Ancient Bloodlord; Twisted Fates. Look for: Rank Rat and Campus Crier as early creatures you can profit from losing.

BLUE / RED · UR

Chandra · noncreature spells

Signposts: Clash of Elements; Saheeli, Jewel of Avishkar. Look for: cheap interaction such as Unsummon and No Admittance to trigger spell payoffs.

BLACK / GREEN · BG

Garruk · creature value

Signposts: Hapatra, the Desert Fang; Primal Witchstalker. Look for: creatures with useful enters effects and Rewrite Regrets to reuse them.

RED / WHITE · RW

Ajani · army and counters

Signposts: Mabel, Valley Hero; Warrior's Blades. Look for: Cadet makers such as Hexhaven Battalion and Heartstring Puller.

GREEN / BLUE · GU

Jace · planeswalker token

Signposts: Kiora of Salt and Sand; Mind Meanderer. Look for: empower Jace, Countersculpt, Inspired Tethermage.

Mana they leave open

Key tricks and interaction to recognize first. Filter by opponent's color; cost includes the colored mana shown.

1W
Combat trick

Academic Ascent

+2/+2 and flying; also empower Jace 2. Before blockers, flying can get through; after blockers, the boost can save a creature.

G
Combat trick

Tethermage's Advantage

+2/+2 at one mana. Respect a single untapped Forest in creature combat.

1R
Combat trick

Blazing Crescendo

+3/+1; exiles the top card to play until the end of their next turn. Big damage push with card access.

U
Tempo

Unsummon

Bounces a creature. Watch for a lost Aura, counter buildup, or a double block that becomes unfavorable.

1B
Removal

Last Gasp

−3/−3 at instant speed. Small attackers and blockers can disappear before damage.

1G
Removal

Compel Brutality

At instant speed, their creature can deal damage equal to its power to your creature or planeswalker. Removal can happen before combat damage.

2B
Removal

Break Under Pressure

They make you sacrifice a creature or planeswalker tied for greatest mana value; they gain 2 life. It targets the player, so hexproof does not save the permanent.

2R
Removal

Fulminous Forte

Instant-speed 5-damage threat. Red can punish a large block or remove a key creature before attacks.

Also check multicolor mana: the ten college and character pairs include modal Charms and other interaction. Read open mana as a warning, especially when an opponent has prepared spells available on creatures.

Rules and table traps

Empower Jace

If you have no Jace token, create one and add loyalty. Its −1 surveils 1; its −3 draws a card. A targeted empower spell that loses every legal target will not resolve or empower.

Prepared creatures

The spell on a prepared creature is a castable copy. It still costs mana and follows its printed timing. Remove the creature before the spell is cast to take away that option.

Heartwood

Heartwood is an artifact token that taps for red or green. The token is itself red and green even if an early printed token uses a colorless-looking frame.

Echoed pairs

Normal and alternate versions appear together in boosters. Compare the actual text: familiar character names may now have different colors or jobs.

Removal first

Save clean removal for opposing bombs or engines. In sealed, a reliable two-color deck with enough early creatures often beats a fragile pile of splash cards.

Bring the basics

Check your store's start time and registration. Bring sleeves, a pen, and a way to track life; the kit includes a spindown die. Ask the judge whenever a new mechanic is unclear.
